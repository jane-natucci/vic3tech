SECTION 1 TITLE: About this guide
================================================================================
Before a campaign I always want to know: [i]who actually starts ahead?[/i] Who has the tech, the army, the navy, the progressive laws? The game doesn't show that side by side, so I read it out of the game files and put it in one place.

This guide sums up what the 1836 start looks like (game version 1.13.11). If you want to check a specific country, I also made a free fan site with all of it: [url=https://vic3tech.jane.berlin]vic3tech.jane.berlin[/url] -- the rest of the guide shows how to use it, including on your own save.

All numbers come straight from the game's files. Starting laws were also checked law by law against a save made at the very start of a new game: every law of all 443 countries matches.

================================================================================
SECTION 2 TITLE: The 1836 start in numbers
================================================================================
All counts cover the 443 countries that exist on 1 January 1836 -- every one that owns land, not only the ones you'd pick to play: 158 of them are decentralized nations and 117 are subjects (puppets, colonies, protectorates and so on). "Europe" means the 64 whose capital is in Europe.

[h2]Technology[/h2]
There are 179 technologies. The game gives every country one of 7 starting tech packages ("tiers"); only 5 countries get the top one:
[list]
[*][b]Great Britain[/b] starts with the most: 54 techs.
[*][b]Belgium, France, Prussia and the USA[/b] follow with 52 each.
[*]In military tech they're all level: 16 each.
[/list]
Most of the world starts far behind: 127 countries are in tier 4, and 171 in tiers 6 and 7.

Battalion counts aren't the whole story. China's 485 are 410 Irregular Infantry and 75 Hussars; Irregular Infantry has 10 offense and 10 defense, against 20/25 for the Line Infantry most of Russia's and Austria's army is, and 25/35 for the Skirmish Infantry of France, Britain and Prussia. (The 1836 files give battalion counts, not how many men are in them.)

[h2]Laws[/h2]
Out of 443 countries (and 64 in Europe):
[list]
[*][b]Mercantilism[/b] is the norm: 258 countries, 57 of 64 in Europe. Only 6 start with [b]Free Trade[/b] -- the free cities of Bremen, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Krakow and Lübeck, plus Oregon -- and just 3 with [b]Protectionism[/b]: France, Great Britain and the USA. (Britain's line in the game files carries the developers' own note: "Corn Laws".)
[*][b]Monarchy[/b] rules 227 countries; [b]Presidential Republic[/b] 32, [b]Parliamentary Republic[/b] only 3.
[*][b]Autocracy[/b] is the starting point for 346 countries. Some voting at all is rare: Wealth Voting 19, Census Voting 8, Landed Voting 5 -- and nobody starts with Universal Suffrage.
[*][b]Serfdom[/b]: 291 countries worldwide, but only 2 in Europe.
[*][b]Slavery Banned[/b]: 155 countries, 55 of them in Europe.
[*][b]Professional Army[/b] in 54 of 64 European countries; the rest of the world mostly starts with [b]Peasant Levies[/b] (311).
[/list]

SECTION 3 TITLE: Look up any country: technology
================================================================================
[IMAGE: 1-tech-tree.png]

On [url=https://vic3tech.jane.berlin]the site[/url], pick a country in the sidebar (hover over another to preview it). The Europe / Whole world switch decides who's listed.

The tech tree shows production, military and society by era, with every prerequisite arrow; techs the country has, can research next, and can't yet look different. Click a tech for its effects, what it unlocks, its cost, and which countries start with it. The sidebar ranks countries by techs in the open category.

Every view has its own link, so you can send someone exactly what you're looking at.

SECTION 4 TITLE: Armies, units, economy and trade
================================================================================
[IMAGE: 2-armies.png]

[b]Armies & navies[/b]: battalions, men, army composition and ships for every country, sortable. Hover over a bar for the breakdown.

[IMAGE: 3-units.png]

[b]Units & mobilization[/b]: every land unit, ship and mobilization option by era, its stats and the tech that unlocks it, and whether the selected country can build it.

[IMAGE: 4-economy.png]

[b]Economy[/b]: buildings by sector and resources -- arable land and the maximum levels of mines, logging camps, fisheries and so on, including undiscovered gold, oil and rubber. Useful for picking a country to build around.

[IMAGE: 5-trade.png]

[b]Trade[/b]: treaties article by article with their weekly net, base prices of every good, and trade centers.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 TITLE: Laws
================================================================================
[IMAGE: 6-laws.png]

Every law group with the country's law highlighted and the alternatives next to it; laws that need a tech or another law it doesn't have yet are dimmed. Click a law for the game's description, its effects, the tech it needs and which countries have it. [b]Across countries[/b] shows how common each law of a group is -- that's where the numbers in this guide come from.

================================================================================
SECTION 6 TITLE: Your own campaign
================================================================================
Every tab can show your game instead of 1836: techs, armies, buildings, treaties, world market prices, trade centers and laws (with the year each one was enacted).

[olist]
[*]Find your save: [i]Documents / Paradox Interactive / Victoria 3 / save games[/i], a .v3 file.
[*]Saves are binary, so first open it on [url=https://pdx.tools]pdx.tools[/url] and click [b]Melt[/b]. That gives you a readable copy ending in _melted.v3.
[*]Drop that file onto the site (or click "Load your save…").
[/olist]

The save is read in your browser and isn't uploaded; even a 360 MB late-game save takes only a second or two. [b]Share this save[/b] makes a link others can open, phones included -- only a small summary is uploaded for that, never the file. Click the date at the top left to reopen saves you loaded before.
